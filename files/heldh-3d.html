HELDH(C)@NBC — MgAlZnNiCo high-entropy LDH on N-doped biochar

Brucite-like [M(OH)6] octahedral layers sharing five cations, with CO32− and H2O in the interlayer galleries, grown on an N-doped biochar support.
Why does HELDH@NBC capture PFHxA? (proposed)
  1. Electrostatic attraction — Al3+ in the hydroxide layers gives them a permanent positive charge, while PFHxA (a strong perfluorinated acid) is present as the carboxylate anion C5F11COO− in water.
  2. Anion exchange & surface binding — the carboxylate head can exchange with loosely held anions at layer surfaces and edges and hydrogen-bond to surface M–OH groups.
  3. Hydrophobic / fluorophilic contact — the C5F11 tail prefers non-polar surfaces such as the carbon network of biochar.
  4. N-doped biochar — N sites add basic/positive sites and electrical conductivity, so binding events reach the electrode.
  5. Sensing signal — PFHxA at the interface changes interfacial charge transfer, read out by EIS (Rct) with a redox probe.
Mechanism under investigation; schematic not to scale.
